# TeaCrafters policy sheet (the only facts the bot may state)

TeaCrafters is an artisan tea subscription. One box ships on the 1st of every month.

## Subscription
- Change tea preferences, skip a month, pause, cancel, or change the shipping address in 'Subscription Settings'.
- All of those changes must be made at least 7 days before the next shipment.
- Pause for up to 3 months at a time.
- Plans: Monthly ($24/box) or Annual (save 15%, billed once a year).
- Teas included: black, green, white, oolong, herbal and specialty blends, curated to your preferences.
- Caffeine-free: herbal teas; tick 'Caffeine-free only' in 'Subscription Settings'.

## Shipping
- A tracking email goes out when the box ships; track it on our website or the courier's site.
- Shipping is free in the US. International shipping goes to select countries; costs and times are on the 'Shipping Information' page.
- An email a few days before shipping lists the teas in the next box; the account page shows it too.

## Payment
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Update the card in 'Payment Methods'.
- If a payment fails, we retry after 3 days and email you; the box ships once payment succeeds.

## Returns and problems
- Returns within 30 days of delivery, unopened and in the original packaging. Contact support to start one.
- A damaged box or a missing tea: send a photo to support within 14 days and we send a free replacement.
- Don't like a tea? Tell us and we adjust future preferences.

## Gifts
- Buy a gift subscription with the 'Gift Subscription' option (3, 6 or 12 months). We email the recipient on the date you pick.

## Teas
- Sourced directly from small, sustainable farms; ethical sourcing.
- Best within 6–12 months; store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
- Feedback: leave a review in the 'Customer Reviews' section of the product page.

## Support
- Email support@teacrafters.com or call 1-800-TEA-CRAFT.

# Lumora Analytics — Employee Handbook

*Lumora Analytics is a fictional company created for the AI Engineer Bootcamp. Any resemblance to a
real company is coincidental.*

## 1. Welcome to Lumora

Lumora Analytics builds forecasting software for mid-sized logistics companies. Our customers use
Lumora to predict shipment volumes, staff their warehouses, and plan delivery routes. We are a team of
about 180 people spread across three hubs (Lisbon, Toronto and Austin) plus remote employees in eleven
countries.

This handbook explains how we work, what you can expect from the company, and what the company expects
from you. It is a living document. The People Operations team reviews it every quarter, and the version
in the company wiki is always the one that applies. If something in this handbook conflicts with your
employment contract or with local law, your contract and local law win.

If you can't find an answer here, ask in the #ask-people channel or email people@lumora.example.
Questions about expenses go to finance@lumora.example, and IT or security questions go to the
#it-help channel.

## 2. Our Values

We hire, review and promote against four values.

**Customers first, with evidence.** We make decisions using data from real customer usage, not opinions
in a meeting. If you propose a change, bring the numbers.

**Write it down.** Decisions, designs and post-mortems are written so that people in other time zones
can follow them. A decision that only exists in a call did not happen.

**Own the outcome.** If you ship it, you watch it in production. Teams own their services end to end,
including on-call.

**Be kind and direct.** Give feedback early, privately and specifically. Assume good intent, and say
what you mean.

## 3. Working Hours and Location

### 3.1 Core hours

Lumora is a flexible-hours company. Every team agrees on at least three hours of overlap per day, called
core hours, when meetings may be scheduled. Outside core hours, you organise your own time. Teams publish
their core hours in their team charter.

### 3.2 Remote and hybrid work

Employees attached to a hub are expected in the office two days per week, chosen by the team. Fully
remote employees are expected to visit a hub or attend a team offsite at least once per year, and the
company pays for that trip under the travel policy in section 7.

You may work from a country other than your country of employment for up to 30 calendar days per year.
Longer stays need written approval from People Operations, because they can create tax and legal
obligations for you and for the company.

### 3.3 Home office setup

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 750 USD (or the local equivalent) to buy a
desk, chair, monitor or other equipment. Claim it through the expense tool within your first 90 days.
Equipment bought with the allowance belongs to you. Laptops are provided separately by IT and remain
company property.

## 4. Time Off

### 4.1 Vacation

Full-time employees get 20 vacation days per year, starting on their first day. New hires receive the
full 20 days in their first calendar year, prorated by the number of full months remaining in the year.

Unused vacation days roll over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Days above that limit expire
on 31 March of the following year. We would rather you take your time off than lose it, and managers
are asked to check in with anyone who has more than 10 unused days by October.

Request vacation in the HR system at least two weeks in advance for blocks of three days or more. For
shorter absences, one week's notice is enough. Your manager approves requests; if a request is declined,
your manager must explain why in writing and suggest alternative dates.

### 4.2 Public holidays

You get the public holidays of the country where you are employed. In addition, the whole company closes
for two "Lumora days": the last working day of June and the last working day of December. Nobody is
expected to be online on Lumora days except the on-call rotation, who receive a replacement day.

### 4.3 Sick leave

There is no fixed limit on sick days. If you are unwell, tell your manager as early as you can and rest.
For absences longer than five consecutive working days, we ask for a doctor's note, mainly so that
People Operations can help with any longer-term support you may need.

### 4.4 Parental leave

All parents, regardless of gender or how they became a parent, receive 18 weeks of fully paid parental
leave, to be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. This is in addition to any
statutory leave. Parents returning from leave may work at 80 percent of their usual hours for full pay
during their first four weeks back.

### 4.5 Other leave

You may take up to 5 days of paid compassionate leave after the death of a close family member, and up
to 2 paid days per year to volunteer for a registered charity. Jury duty and other civic obligations
are paid in full.

## 5. Pay and Benefits

### 5.1 Pay schedule

Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th, or on the previous working day when the 25th falls on a weekend
or public holiday. Payslips are available in the HR system.

### 5.2 Salary reviews

Salaries are reviewed once a year, in March, based on the performance review cycle in section 9 and on
market data for each role and location. Promotions can happen at any time and come with a salary
review.

### 5.3 Health benefits

Lumora provides private health insurance in every country where it is not already provided by the
state at a comparable level. Coverage includes partners and dependent children at no extra cost. Mental
health support, including up to 12 therapy sessions per year, is included for every employee.

### 5.4 Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,200 USD per year for courses, books, conferences and
certifications related to their work. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel paid from the
learning budget follows the travel policy, but the travel cost itself is paid from the team's travel
budget, not from your learning budget.

You may also spend up to four working days per year on learning, such as attending a conference or
completing a course, without using vacation days.

## 6. Equipment and IT

### 6.1 Laptops

IT issues every employee a laptop, which is refreshed every three years. Laptops are managed by IT:
disk encryption, automatic updates and endpoint protection must stay enabled. Do not install software
from unknown sources. If you need a tool that is not in the self-service catalogue, request it in
#it-help.

### 6.2 Phones

Lumora does not provide phones by default. If your role requires you to be reachable outside working
hours, such as on-call engineers, you may claim a phone allowance of 30 USD per month through the
expense tool.

### 6.3 Lost or stolen equipment

Report lost or stolen equipment to #it-help and to security@lumora.example within one hour of noticing.
IT will lock and wipe the device remotely. You will not be blamed for losing a device, but a late report
is treated as a security incident.

## 7. Expenses and Travel

### 7.1 General rules

Lumora pays for reasonable costs you incur while doing your job. "Reasonable" means what a sensible
person would spend if it were their own money. Submit every expense in the expense tool within 30 days,
with a photo of the receipt. Expenses submitted more than 90 days late are not eligible for
reimbursement.

Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly salary when the claim is approved by the 15th of the month.
Claims approved after the 15th are paid the following month.

### 7.2 Meals

When you travel for work, meals are covered up to a daily limit of 75 USD per person, or the local
equivalent. The limit applies to the whole day, not per meal. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at team
events approved in advance by a director.

Team meals at the office, for example during an offsite or a release celebration, may be expensed up to
40 USD per person with your manager's approval. Client dinners are covered up to 120 USD per person and
must list every attendee in the expense claim.

### 7.3 Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal so that we can find you in an emergency.
Economy class is the default. For flights longer than eight hours, premium economy is allowed. Business
class needs approval from your vice president.

Hotels are covered up to the nightly cap shown in the travel portal for each city. If you book outside
the portal, you must still respect the cap, and your claim for reimbursement must include the hotel's
itemised invoice.

You can use taxis or ride-hailing services to and from airports and for late-night travel. Otherwise,
use public transport where it is practical. Car rental requires your manager's approval.

### 7.4 Mileage

If you use your own car for work trips, Lumora pays mileage at the official tax-free rate of your
country of employment. Commuting to your regular office does not count as a work trip.

### 7.5 Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times per year may request a corporate card. Corporate cards must
not be used for personal purchases, even if you intend to repay them. Card statements must be reconciled
in the expense tool every month.

### 7.6 What is never reimbursed

The following are not eligible for reimbursement: fines and penalties, including parking and speeding
tickets; personal entertainment such as films or streaming during travel; upgrades you choose for
comfort; costs for family members who travel with you; and lost personal items.

## 8. Security and Data Protection

### 8.1 Accounts and passwords

Use the company password manager for every work account. Single sign-on is required for every tool that
supports it. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory, and hardware security keys are issued to
everyone with production access. Never share your credentials, even with IT.

### 8.2 Data classification

We classify data in four levels.

- **Public:** information already published on our website or in marketing material.
- **Internal:** information for Lumora employees only, such as this handbook or team plans.
- **Confidential:** customer data, financial results before publication, contracts and employee
  records. Confidential data may only be stored in approved systems.
- **Restricted:** credentials, encryption keys and personal data of customers' end users. Access is
  granted per person, logged, and reviewed every quarter.

### 8.3 Using AI tools

You may use the AI assistants approved by the security team for internal and public data. Confidential
or restricted data may only be used with tools that have a signed data processing agreement and that
appear on the approved list in the wiki. Never paste customer data, credentials or source code from
restricted repositories into a tool that is not on the approved list. Output from AI tools must be
reviewed by a person before it is sent to a customer or merged into production code.

### 8.4 Reporting incidents

If you suspect a security incident, such as a phishing email you clicked, a leaked credential or
unusual access to customer data, report it immediately to security@lumora.example or through the
#security-incident channel. Report first, investigate later. Nobody is disciplined for reporting an
honest mistake quickly.

## 9. Performance and Growth

### 9.1 Review cycle

We run two performance conversations per year, in March and September. Each one includes a written
self-review, feedback from two to four peers, and a written review from your manager. The March review
also feeds the salary review in section 5.2.

### 9.2 One-to-ones

Every employee has a one-to-one with their manager at least every two weeks. The employee owns the
agenda. Managers keep short written notes that both people can see.

### 9.3 Career levels

Each job family has published career levels with clear expectations. You can see the expectations for
every level in the wiki, including levels above your own. Promotions are decided by a calibration group,
not by a single manager, to keep decisions consistent across teams.

### 9.4 Internal moves

After 12 months in your role, you may apply for open roles in other teams. Your current manager is
informed when you reach the final interview stage, not before.

## 10. Engineering Practices

### 10.1 Code review

Every change to production code needs at least one approving review from someone other than the author.
Changes to authentication, billing or data deletion need two approvals, one of them from the owning
team.

### 10.2 On-call

Engineering teams that own production services run a weekly on-call rotation. On-call engineers receive
an allowance of 150 USD per week, plus one day off in lieu for any week in which they were paged outside
working hours more than three times. Nobody is on call for more than one week in four.

### 10.3 Incidents and post-mortems

Customer-facing incidents get a written post-mortem within five working days. Post-mortems are
blameless: they describe what happened, why the system allowed it, and what we will change. They are
shared with the whole company.

### 10.4 Release schedule

We release to production every working day except Fridays after 14:00 local time of the owning team,
and during the two weeks around the end-of-year Lumora day. Emergency fixes are always allowed.

## 11. Conduct

### 11.1 Respect

Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated, whether in the office, online, at company
events or with customers. Report concerns to your manager, to People Operations, or anonymously through
the ethics line listed in the wiki.

### 11.2 Conflicts of interest

Tell People Operations about any outside work, investments or relationships that could conflict with
your work at Lumora. Most side projects are fine; we just need to know about them.

### 11.3 Gifts

You may accept gifts from customers or suppliers worth up to 50 USD. Anything above that must be
declared to finance, who will decide whether you may keep it.

## 12. Leaving Lumora

### 12.1 Notice

Notice periods are set in your contract. During the notice period, we will agree a handover plan with
your manager.

### 12.2 Final pay and expenses

Your final salary includes payment for unused vacation days, calculated according to local law. Submit
any outstanding expense claims before your last day; claims submitted after you leave cannot be
processed through the expense tool and must be emailed to finance@lumora.example for manual
reimbursement within 30 days.

### 12.3 Returning equipment

Return your laptop and any security keys on or before your last day. IT will send a prepaid courier
label to remote employees.
